Privacy notice

This notice covers this pre-release website and describes the planned mobile release. Store release and production advertising are not yet active. The public site is maintained through the GitHub account cv2pw47rsz-crypto. The seller identity and private support contact will be added before launch.

Your game progress

Hurry Birdy! does not require a game account. Scores, coins, bird and hat choices, settings, the coarse age group and an ad-free ownership cache are stored on your device. The game does not transmit your entered age to a game server or retain your exact age. We do not operate a gameplay backend or install a separate analytics service. Deleting the app or clearing its data can remove local progress.

Ads and privacy choices

The mobile game integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent. Ads are off for unknown-age and under-13 players. For eligible players, the game requests contextual, non-personalized ads and turns off publisher first-party ad IDs. The iOS game does not request permission to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The Android build removes advertising-ID permission. Contextual ads still involve data processing.

When the ad service is used, Google may process an IP address and approximate location, app/ad interactions, device or app identifiers, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, protect against fraud and operate the service. Google handles this data under its own policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and advertising information.

Consent is requested where required before ad requests. You can revisit Ad privacy choices and Age & privacy from Options → Ad-free & privacy. You can play when you decline consent, when no ad is available or when you are offline. Buying Fly Ad-Free removes every ad and rewarded-video offer.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes purchases using your store account. The game receives product, purchase and entitlement information to verify and restore access. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase restoration uses the same platform’s account; it is not a cross-platform transfer. The store retains transaction information under its policies.

Children

The release is intended for teens and adults. The in-game age choice is neutral and optional. Under-13 and unknown-age players can play with ads disabled. Store audience and content-rating declarations will reflect the actual game.

This website and support

This website uses no advertising cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts or embedded social-media players. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to other services open sites with their own policies.

Messages submitted to the public support tracker are public. Do not submit personal, financial or account details there. Private support details will be published before launch. Store billing and refund issues can be handled directly with Apple or Google.

Control and retention

Clear the app’s local data to remove locally stored progress and preferences. This does not cancel a store purchase or delete records held by Apple, Google or GitHub. Use each provider’s privacy controls and policies for their retained information. Updated practices will be reflected in this notice before the affected release.
