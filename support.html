Support

The iPhone and Android release is being prepared. These instructions describe the upcoming mobile version.

How do I remove ads?

Open Options → Ad-free & privacy. The store shows your local price before you confirm. Fly Ad-Free is a one-time purchase. It removes between-run ads and rewarded-video offers. After a run that earned coins, claim the extra coins without a video.

I already purchased. How do I restore it?

Sign into the same Apple or Google Play store account, connect to the internet, then select Restore Purchases on the ad-free screen. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google. A pending payment unlocks only after the store confirms it.

Where are my coins or high score?

Progress is stored on this device. It is not synchronized between devices. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data can remove local progress. Restoring the ad-free purchase does not restore scores, coins or hats.

Why is there no rewarded video?

A video may be unavailable because you are offline, no ad is ready, or your privacy/age settings prevent it. Replay stays available. A reward is added only when the ad service confirms completion, once per run.

How do I change privacy choices?

Open Options → Ad-free & privacy for Age & privacy and Ad privacy choices. Skipping the age choice keeps ads off. Gameplay remains available when consent is declined.

Purchases, billing and refunds

Payments are processed by your platform’s store. For billing or refund requests use Apple’s purchase support or Google Play’s refund help. Never post receipts, passwords or payment details publicly.

Pre-release feedback

You can report a general game issue on the public support tracker. Include the device model, operating system, game version and what happened. Do not include personal information. For private support, contact deskdungeongames@gmail.com.
